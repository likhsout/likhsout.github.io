01 — About me

I like software that stays out of the way.

I'm Likhon — a software engineering student at Daffodil International University in Bangladesh. I got here by taking things apart: first customising Android, then ReVanced patches and bytecode tweaks, and eventually building my own apps instead of only modifying other people's.

Today I build Flutter and native Android apps (Dart & Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3), web tools and sites (React, TypeScript, plain HTML/CSS/JS) and browser extensions on Manifest V3.

I also write Python automations for the boring parts of student life — like turning messy university routine spreadsheets into clean, structured JSON with pandas and openpyxl.

My rule is simple — small, fast and private. Offline-first where it makes sense, no bloat, no telemetry, no surprises.
